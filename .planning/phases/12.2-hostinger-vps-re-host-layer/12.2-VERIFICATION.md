This means any future EasyPanel-triggered restartService/deployService of this specific service will silently drop the mount, requiring a manual reapplication. This was explicitly escalated to and accepted by the user on 2026-07-31 (documented in 12.2-01-SUMMARY.md's Escalation section and in STATE.md's Open Items). Per this verification's scope, this is treated as a known, accepted operational risk and is not flagged as a gap.
